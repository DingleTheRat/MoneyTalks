A unique Economy mod for Minecraft, designed for keep-inventory servers. Money (Coins) is obtained via the investor villager. The villager uses the Merchant's Carpet as a work station. If you don't wanna clog up your inventory, feel free to craft a Wallet. The mod aims to discourage death by making you drop your hard-earned coins upon death, even if your wallet is in a chest.

![Border](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/fa0324ca7f9246dfa1b98170e654deb69ef9043e.png)

## ⭐ Features:
- **Coins** - The currency of the mod, mainly used with the investor villager and Merchant's Carpets. If keep-inventory is on, the player will drop 20% of their coins upon death via a player. You cannot remove this item from your inventory.
- **Wallet** - An item that behaves kinda like the bundle, but can only store coins, allowing you to move coins around. Crafted with 5 leather in a U shape. It has no limit and decreases the amount of coins dropped upon death to 15%. More info below.
- **Merchant's Carpet** - A thicker version of the carpet obtained by placing 4 wool in a 2x2 square in a crafting table. It can be re-dyed with any dye. It allows you to sell items to other players. More info below.
- **Investor villager** - Quite an OP villager that sells goodies such as Netherite Ingots. He is how you get coins. Get him by placing a Merchant's Carpet as a work-station. More info below.
- **Trimmed Hopper** - An elegant hopper that can be linked to a chest to deposit all its contents in. It's crafted with a hopper in the middle and two columns of diamonds on its sides. More info below.
- (✨ New!) **Doubloon Compressor** - A functional block crafted with a netherite ingot, soulsand at the bottom row, and deepslate elsewhere. Once fueled with blaze powder, right-click on it with a wallet to turn all of its contents into doubloons (Rate: 10 coins/doubloon, 1 blaze powder lasts for 3 doubloons). Note: Only the placer can open its UI.
- (✨ New!) **Doubloon** - A placable form of the coin obtained via the doubloon compressor, and can be crafted back into 10 coins. Like coins, it cannot be stored, and it is always dropped upon death. Once placed, it glows, giving away its location to others. Make sure to protect it!

## 💼 The Wallet:
This item lets you move coins into containers (chests & stuff) by putting them inside it, kinda like the bundle. It's crafted with 5 leather in a U shape. Every Wallet has an owner, the person who had it last. Upon the death of the owner, the wallet will drop 15% of what it contains wherever the owner died. This also means the amount will be deducted from your wallet. This only happens if a player kills the owner, and it doesn't matter if keep-inventory is on or not (in fact, this is meant to be played with keep-inventory).
</details>

## 🧶 The Merchant's Carpet
### General
A very thick carpet that comes in all dye colors by placing 4 wool in a 2x2 square in a crafting table, and can be re-dyed by crafting it with any dye. The person who places it down is now the owner, and they're the only one who can modify its properties. When someone buys something, it will notify you with an actionbar message notifying them about the details of the purchase. Non-owners will find it to be a very hard to break the carpet, but for the owner, it's one click and all the items input will fall out.
### How to set up
To set up, place down the carpet and put any amount of coins in it (it has to be coins, and they will be deducted) - that will be the price. If you see a floating coin appear, the price is set. Place the item you want to sell next; the amount you enter there determines how much you will get per purchase, and it will also be added to the inventory. Now, you can't modify what you set; you can only buy from yourself or restock the carpet with the same item. If the carpet runs out of stock, the coin will appear again to notify you. The less stock there is, the slower the item will spin. The more, the faster.
### How to buy
The block does not show prices, so the cost-to-item ratio should be shown on a sign. Interact with the block with a coin, and the amount will be deducted from your balance. The stuff you bought will pop out, but be warned, if you don't pick it up, someone can steal it.
### Collecting the money
There are two main ways to collect money from your carpet: right-clicking on it with an empty hand or linking it to a Wallet. To link your carpet to a wallet, simply right-click on it with the Wallet of your choice, and it will become enchanted. Now your wallet will auto-collect money from any purchase, even if you're offline.
</details>

## 💎 Trimmed Hopper
You can craft this by placing two columns of diamonds on the left and right, and a hopper in the middle. This hopper can be linked to a chest by right-clicking a chest with it. If the link was successful, you should hear a chain sound, the hopper becoming enchanted, and a tooltip notifying which chest it's linked to. Now, upon placing it down, anything that goes into it will immediately go to the linked chest (as long as there is room and it's not destroyed).
</details>

- Coin -> 2 Golden Carrots (10 uses)
- 5 Coins -> Golden Apple (10 uses)
- Coin -> 4 Wind Charges (8 uses)
### Level #5 (random, 2 of 4)
- Netherite Ingot -> 36 Coins (1 use)
- 64 Coins -> Netherite Ingot (1 use)
- 32 Coins -> Totem of Undying (2 uses)
- 40 Coins -> Enchanted Golden Apple (2 uses)

- Random - only some of the listed trades show up on each Investor (e.g. "2 of 4").
